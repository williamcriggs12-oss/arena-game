Controls and tips

WASD move · Space jump, Space again in air = double jump · hold W beside a wall to wall-run, Space to wall-jump · C slide · right-click aim · Esc reopens this menu

Bleed out: when your health hits zero you go down for 15 seconds. You crawl slowly and fight with a sidearm. A teammate (Team Deathmatch or Waves) can revive you by standing close and holding E for 3 seconds. Enemy hits finish you off, and if the timer runs out you die.

Movement: you sprint automatically (hold Shift to walk). Wall-running gets faster the longer you hold the wall, and a wall-jump keeps that speed. Jump on the frame you land, or hold Space, to keep momentum. A second jump in mid-air gains height but sheds speed. C slides, and jumping out of a slide carries it. Jump into a ledge to mantle onto it. F inspects your weapon.

Mech: fighting fills the meter at the bottom (damage and kills fill it faster). At 100% press X to call Mechdrop, and you climb in when it lands. In the Mech: Q dashes, X ejects. V toggles third person any time.

Grenades: G throws, keys 1-4 switch type (frag, flashbang, smoke, sticky). You carry 2 (5 if smoke is selected) and one recharges every 7 seconds (4 for smoke). Frags and sticky bombs knock you around too, so look away from flashbangs.

Tether: Q hooks the surface you look at, then you swing on the rope. A/D/W/S pump the swing, hold E to reel in, hold R to let the rope out, Space or Q to let go and fly.

Campaign (co-op): press CAMPAIGN for six story missions with cut-scenes. Anyone in the Campaign lobby plays the same mission together, and enemies get tougher with every player who joins. The squad shares a pool of reinforcements, and downed teammates can be revived with E. Your progress is saved on this device.
